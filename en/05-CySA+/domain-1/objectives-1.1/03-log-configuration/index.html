Log Configuration

DEFINITION

Log Configuration

Log configuration determines what events are recorded, how much detail is captured, and where logs are sent.

LEVEL

Debug

Maximum diagnostic detail.

LEVEL

Info / Warning

Operational activity and conditions requiring attention.

LEVEL

Error / Critical

Failures and severe conditions.

BALANCE

Signal vs Noise

Too little reduces visibility; too much increases noise and storage.

VISUAL MODEL

Choosing the right logging level

More detail increases visibility, but also volume and noise.

Exam Tip: Too little logging reduces visibility; too much logging increases noise and storage needs.
